Home

Hello! I'm a first year Computer Science student, and this is my personal website.

Here I'll create regular blog posts, showcase my portfolio, and display my resume for internet passerby.

Why make a website?

It's important that we keep in mind that the Internet is made up of real people. Many websites in the modern day exist purely to sell a product or promote a company, and too few allow people to truly express themselves. By creating my own website, I can create a space that is entirely of my own design. Creativity is what makes us human, after all.

Why computer science?

I've always been entranced by computers. The concept that we can create absolutely anything through computers has been my constant inspiration as I learn more about CS and conduct more work in the space. Software engineering in particular sparks my interest because it often blends deep programming with human collaboration, two things I enjoy greatly.

I pursue computer science because I love computer science!
